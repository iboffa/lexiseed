# Polynesian reference sheet (codebook v1)

Languages (6): Maori, Hawaiian, Samoan, Tongan, Tahitian, Rapanui. PHOIBLE has inventories only for Maori (SPA 42), Hawaiian (SPA 43, chosen over UPSID 352) and Tongan (PH 1086); Samoan, Tahitian and Rapanui PHOIBLE language pages returned 404 twice and PHOIBLE name searches were empty, so their phoneme fields are null. No historical member (Proto-Polynesian is not a WALS/PHOIBLE language); `historical` false throughout.

## Ranges
- Consonants: 9 (Hawaiian) to 12 (Tongan); Maori 10.
- Vowel qualities: 5 (Tongan) to 6 (Maori, Hawaiian: literal count, because PHOIBLE uses a different symbol for short vs long mid vowels: Maori o̞/oː, Hawaiian ɛ/eː; phonologically 5). Long vowels in Maori and Hawaiian, not in Tongan's PH inventory. No diphthongs listed.
- WALS 1A: Small (Maori, Hawaiian, Rapanui); 2A Average (5-6); 12A Simple; 13A No tones.

## Consonants
- In all 3: p k m h. In 2: w f n ŋ s ʔ.
- Few: t (Maori; Tongan t̪), ɾ (Maori), l (Hawaiian), ɺ (Tongan), v (Tongan), n̪ (Tongan).

## Vowels
- i u in all; a/ɑ, e/ɛ, o/o̞ in all, i.e. a plain five-vowel system, plus length in Maori/Hawaiian.

## Typology (WALS)
- Order: VSO (Maori, Hawaiian, Tahitian, Rapanui), No dominant order (Samoan, Tongan).
- Prepositions (6/6), Noun-Adjective (6/6), Noun-Genitive (5/6; Tahitian No dominant order).
- Little affixation (6/6); fusion Isolating/concatenative (Maori), Exclusively isolating (Rapanui).
- No morphological case-marking (Maori, Rapanui); No case affixes or adpositional clitics (6/6); no gender (None, 3/3).
- Definite word distinct from demonstrative (5/5); indefinite: distinct from 'one' (4) vs same as 'one' (Tahitian, Rapanui).
- Alignment Nominative - accusative (Maori) vs Neutral (Rapanui). Aspect: Grammatical marking (Hawaiian, Tahitian) vs No grammatical marking (Maori, Rapanui). No inflectional future (4/4).

## What varies
- Exact consonant set (t vs k reflexes, r/l, presence of ʔ, s, f, v), vowel length phonemicity, basic word order (VSO vs no dominant), aspect marking.

## Gaps
- Samoan, Tahitian, Rapanui: no PHOIBLE inventory. Samoan, Tongan, Tahitian: WALS phonology (1A, 2A, 12A, 13A) null. Tahitian script null (Wikipedia infobox section has no script line). vowel_harmony null throughout.
