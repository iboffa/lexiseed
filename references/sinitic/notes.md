## Ranges
- Consonants (PHOIBLE): 14 (Fuzhou, UPSID) to 29 (Wu, SPA); Mandarin 27, Cantonese 22, Hakka 21, Min Nan 19. Counts include syllabic nasals/fricatives (m̩ ŋ̩ z̞̩) that PHOIBLE classes as consonants.
- Vowel qualities: 5 to 7 (Mandarin 11 only because SPA lists rhotacised vowels a˞ ə˞ ɤ˞ u˞ etc. as separate segments; without them Mandarin has a i u y ɤ). No long vowels, no phonemic diphthongs listed.
- WALS 1A: mostly Average (4), one Moderately small (Hakka), one Small (Fuzhou). WALS 2A: split Average (5-6) x3 / Large (7-14) x3.
- Tone: all six "Complex tone system" (13A). PHOIBLE SPA inventories list 3-5 tone segments each (kept out of C/V lists); UPSID inventories (Min Nan, Fuzhou) list none.

## Consonants
- In most inventories (>=4 of 6): p pʰ k kʰ m ŋ, t tʰ (UPSID writes them t̪|t, t̪ʰ|tʰ: dental/alveolar unspecified), w j f l h, ts tsʰ (5 of 6 counting UPSID t̪s̪|ts), s, n.
- In few (1-2): voiced/murmured stops (Wu b̤̥ d̤̥ ɡ̤̥, Min Nan b d ɡ dz), retroflex ʈʂ ʈʂʰ ʂ ʐ (Mandarin), palatal cç cçʰ ç (Mandarin), postalveolar t̠ʃ t̠ʃʰ ʃ (Cantonese), labialised kʷ kʷʰ (Cantonese), prenasalised mb nd ŋɡ̟ (Hakka), χ/x, v z ɦ (Wu), l̥.
- Glottal stop ʔ in 3 (Wu, Hakka, Min Nan).

## Vowels
- In all: a i u. Common: ɔ (4), y (3). Few: æ ɛ e o œ ə, nasal vowels only in Wu (ĩ æ̃ õ̞).

## Typology (WALS)
- Order SVO where coded (Mandarin, Cantonese, Hakka); adpositions "No dominant order"; Genitive-Noun and Adjective-Noun (Mandarin, Cantonese).
- No case (Mandarin: No morphological case-marking; Mandarin/Cantonese: no case affixes or adpositional clitics); no gender (None); alignment Neutral (Mandarin).
- Aspect: Grammatical marking; No inflectional future. Affixation: Mandarin "Strongly suffixing" vs Cantonese "Little affixation" (note the disagreement). Fusion: Isolating/concatenative (Mandarin).
- Syllables Moderately complex (5 of 5 coded).

## What varies
- Consonant inventory size and laryngeal contrasts (aspiration only vs Wu/Min voiced or murmured series); vowel count (5 vs 7+); presence of retroflex/palatal series (Mandarin only); ʔ codas.

## Gaps
- Min Nan, Wu, Fuzhou have only phonology features in WALS (12-19 features), so morphosyntax is null for them; Hakka has only 81A among syntax.
- vowel_harmony null throughout (not stated in sources).
- UPSID inventories (481, 276) use PHOIBLE's ambiguous "t̪|t" notation and no tones.
