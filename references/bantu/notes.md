## Ranges
- Consonants: 15 (Lingala, AA, a small orthography-based source) to 75 (Xhosa, GM 1319, includes full click series and murmured/breathy series). Swahili 31, Zulu 35 (SPA), Shona 37, Kinyarwanda 39. Kinyarwanda and Shona list many labialised/palatalised segments (Shona sʷ zʷ; Kinyarwanda ɡʷ kʷ ŋʷ hʷ ɡʲ kʲ hʲ).
- Vowel qualities: 5 (Swahili, Zulu, Shona, Kinyarwanda) to 7 (Lingala, Xhosa: ɛ ɔ added). Long vowels only in Kinyarwanda (PH 911). No diphthongs.
- WALS 1A: Moderately large (Swahili, Zulu); 2A Average (5-6).
- Tone: Swahili "No tones", Zulu "Simple tone system" (others not coded in WALS). PHOIBLE lists tones for Zulu (˥ ˥˦ ˩) and Xhosa (˦ ˨), kept out of C/V lists.

## Consonants
- In all 6: m w j f. In 5: p t k, n ɲ ŋ, s ʃ h. In 4: ɓ, pʰ kʰ, l, z v.
- In 3: ɗ, r, b d, t̠ʃ d̠ʒ, tʰ, ɬ.
- Few: clicks kǀ kǁ kǃ (+ many Xhosa variants) only Zulu and Xhosa; ejectives pʼ tʼ kʼ tsʼ t̠ʃʼ (Zulu, Xhosa); ɮ; prenasalised stops listed as units only in Xhosa (mb nd ŋɡ etc., murmured); ɠ ʄ θ ð (Swahili); β ɣ x.
- Syllabic nasals m̩ n̩ (Swahili, Zulu).

## Vowels
- In all: i u; nearly all: a e o. 7-vowel systems (ɛ ɔ) in Lingala and Xhosa; Zulu SPA writes ɛ ɔ ɒ.

## Typology (WALS)
- SVO (6/6), Prepositions (5/5), Noun-Genitive (5/5), Noun-Adjective (6/6).
- Genders "Five or more" wherever coded (4/4).
- Prefixing: Strong prefixing (Zulu, Shona, Xhosa), Weakly prefixing (Swahili). Fusion Exclusively concatenative.
- No morphological case-marking (3/3); 51A varies: Case prefixes (Zulu, Xhosa), Prepositional clitics (Kinyarwanda), Mixed (Swahili).
- Alignment Neutral (Swahili, Zulu). Inflectional future exists (Swahili, Zulu). Aspect: Zulu Grammatical marking, Swahili No grammatical marking.
- Articles: Demonstrative used as definite (Swahili, Xhosa), none (Zulu).
- Syllables: Simple (Swahili) / Moderately complex (Zulu).

## What varies
- Consonant inventory size driven by clicks, ejectives, breathy series, prenasalisation (southern Nguni: Zulu/Xhosa) vs compact eastern/central systems; 5 vs 7 vowels; tone presence (Swahili toneless).

## Gaps
- Shona, Kinyarwanda, Lingala, Xhosa lack WALS phonology features (1A, 2A, 12A, 13A null). Lingala inventory source is small (AA). vowel_harmony null throughout.
