IPA reader

Every ▶ button on the site uses this reader. The neural voice (Kokoro, an open model that runs in this browser) reads IPA directly and sounds the most natural; the first ▶ downloads it once (about 92 MB). Kokoro learnt eight languages, so Adaptive blends the speakers whose languages sound most like this one, and Neutral blends all of them; sounds none of the eight has are replaced by the nearest one, and tones are only carried by the Mandarin speaker. The built-in voice is a synthesiser written for this page: robotic, but it reads every sound and tone exactly and needs no download. The system voice uses a Spanish, Italian or Portuguese voice installed on your device and reads a Spanish-style respelling.
